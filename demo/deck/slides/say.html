The Assistant

What you can say

Said What happens
“Mrs. Johnson, 42 Elm, garage cleanout Friday morning, 300 cash” Books the job and asks about anything missing
“Johnson’s done, paid cash” Marks her job done and paid
“Picked up dumpster 1, 2.8 tons” Logs the pickup and works out the overweight charge
“Swapped 2 at Keane’s, 3.1 tons, left 5” Takes one away, leaves the other
“Linda Johnson’s new number is 508 555 0199” Updates the customer
“Which dumpsters are due back Friday?” Answers from the records
These are examples, not a fixed list. It also handles cancellations, rescheduling, price changes, call-backs, drop-offs and notes about a dumpster. If it can’t do something, it says so plainly.
