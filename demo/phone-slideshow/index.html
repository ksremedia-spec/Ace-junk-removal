Ace Junk Removal App

One app for the phones and the office: customers, jobs, the six dumpsters, and a voice assistant.

Swipe to go through it, or use the arrows below.Use the arrows below or the arrow keys on the keyboard.

What it covers

One app for the office and the field

Customers and jobs

Every customer and job in one place, with search, what is owed, and each customer’s history.

The six dumpsters

Where each one is, when it is due back, and the overweight charge worked out at pickup.

A voice assistant

Say what happened or what is needed. It makes the change and shows it first.

iPhone and Mac

The crew’s phones and the office Mac work from the same records.

On the iPhone

Today

The day at a glance.

  • How many jobs today, and for how much
  • Dumpsters due back or overdue
  • What is owed, then today’s and tomorrow’s jobs

On the iPhone

Jobs

Upcoming, Unpaid and Done.

  • Upcoming jobs grouped by day, with a count and a total for each day
  • Unpaid lists every finished job not paid yet
  • Swipe a job to mark it done

On the iPhone

Customers

The whole customer list, in your pocket.

  • Search by name, phone number or address
  • Each customer’s jobs, totals and what they owe
  • Call or get directions in one tap

On the iPhone

Dumpsters

All six at a glance.

  • In the yard, out, due today or overdue
  • Where each one is and when it is due back
  • Enter the weight at pickup and the overweight charge is worked out

The Assistant

Say it the way you’d say it on the phone

  • In the office: book jobs, update customers, ask about the records
  • In the field: mark jobs done and paid, log dumpster drop-offs, pickups and swaps with the weight
  • Several things in one breath are fine

The Assistant

What you can say

“Mrs. Johnson, 42 Elm, garage cleanout Friday morning, 300 cash”

Books the job and asks about anything missing

“Johnson’s done, paid cash”

Marks her job done and paid

“Picked up dumpster 1, 2.8 tons”

Logs the pickup and works out the overweight charge

“Swapped 2 at Keane’s, 3.1 tons, left 5”

Takes one away, leaves the other

“Linda Johnson’s new number is 508 555 0199”

Updates the customer

“Which dumpsters are due back Friday?”

Answers from the records

These are examples. It also handles cancellations, moved jobs, price changes, call-backs and notes about a dumpster.
